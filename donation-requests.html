Donation Requests

We love to give back by investing in our community! In order to be considered for a donation, please complete the request form linked below. Once you have completed the form, we will review it for consideration. Please note that we receive numerous requests each month, so donation requests are not guaranteed.

  • In order to be considered, you must apply for donations 45 days in advance.
  • We only donate to Non-Profits
  • Discounts are only available for non-profits that meet the state of Minnesota requirements for prepared food resale, and are pre-approved by our specific location.

If your request is accepted, you will receive an email from our team.

We look forward to partnering with you!
