Team Member Benefits

We invest in our team. Here's what working at Chick-fil-A Maple Grove looks like.

  • Team Member & Family Discount
    • 50% off meals for you and your family, even when not working
    • Eligibility tied to current CEM goals posted on the Focus Board
    • See a leader for current goal status
  • Meal Allowance
    • Up to $11.50 allowance provided every shift
    • Must include a medium drink at the start of your shift
    • Drink must be kept in the designated location
  • Paid Time Off
    • Available after 2 or more years of employment
    • Must maintain full-time hours (32+ hrs/week) to qualify
    • Eligible time will appear on your paycheck stub
  • Serve With a Purpose
    • Volunteer opportunities with local Maple Grove non-profits
    • We arrange time for you to give back to the community
    • Talk to a leader to express interest
  • 401(k) Plan
    • Eligible after 12 months of employment
    • Dollar-for-dollar match up to 3% of your paycheck
    • Submit an expression of interest form to enroll
  • Grow With Us
    • Access to books on leadership, motivation, and financial literacy
    • Resources for personal growth beyond your CFA career
    • Ask a leader about checking materials out
