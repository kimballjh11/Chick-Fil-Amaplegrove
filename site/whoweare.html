Who We Are

A locally owned restaurant with a passion for people and community.

Meet Our Operator

Nancy and Jeff (spouse and all-around great guy) moved to Minnesota from Georgia in the summer of 2013 to open Chick-fil-A in Maple Grove, one of the first free standing location in the state. Both are proud Air Force veterans, who love this country and were honored to serve.

Nancy was born in Indiana but raised around the world. As an Army brat she lived in several states, Germany and South Korea. Nancy loves sports but rarely plays them in her old age. She is also an ordained Lutheran pastor who enjoys helping people understand the grace of God and God’s love for all. She has a heart for people. Jeff was born in Ohio but raised in Georgia. He loves sports too but like Nancy he doesn’t play much anymore, just watches. Both are big SEC football fans. Go DAWGS! Jeff is a retired Delta pilot who now helps in the restaurant with maintenance and other stuff.

Jeff and Nancy have two children, twins Jon and Jamie, born in San Antonio, Texas. Jon lives with his partner Joe in Berlin Germany and works for the German company, SAP. Jamie lives with her husband Jeremy in Savage, Minnesota. She works as a transplant nurse at Abbot Northwestern Hospital. They have a son, Henry, who loves visiting Crea and G (Jeff and Nancy) at their house on the river in Clearwater. He enjoys riding his John Deere and doing yardwork.

Our History

Chick-fil-A was founded in 1967 by Truett Cathy in Hapeville, Georgia — with one simple idea: serve the best chicken sandwich anyone had ever tasted. Since then, Chick-fil-A has grown to thousands of locations nationwide, each one locally operated and deeply connected to its community.

Our Maple Grove location carries on that legacy every day — one guest, one meal, one moment at a time.
