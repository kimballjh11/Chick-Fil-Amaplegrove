The More You Visit, the More You Earn

Chick-fil-A One is our free rewards program. Earn points on every qualifying purchase and redeem them for free food starting at just 200 points.

Member

Free to join

Earn 10 points per $1 spent on qualifying orders. Rewards start at 200 points and include free food like an Icedream® cone, Waffle Potato Fries®, and more.

Silver

Unlocks at 1,000 points

Earn 11 points per $1. Includes a free birthday reward, the ability to gift rewards to others, and backstage tour access at Chick-fil-A Home Office for up to 5 guests.

Red

Unlocks at 4,000 points

Earn 12 points per $1. Includes all Silver benefits plus more ways to earn and redeem toward the food you love.

Signature

Unlocks at 10,000 points

Earn 13 points per $1. Access exclusive rewards unavailable to any other tier, plus all benefits from every level below.
