The More You Visit, the More You Earn

Chick-fil-A One is our free rewards program. Every dollar you spend earns you points — and points unlock free food, exclusive offers, and more.

Member

Start earning 10 points per $1 spent the moment you sign up. Redeem for free food and treats.

Silver

Reach Silver status and earn 11 points per $1. Plus unlock a free birthday reward.

Red & Signature

Our top tiers earn even more points and get access to exclusive perks and member-only offers.
