Why Work Here?

We invest in our team members the same way we invest in our guests, with genuine care and a commitment to helping you succeed.

  • Competitive Pay — We offer competitive wages and performance-based raises to reward great work.
  • Sundays Off — Every Chick-fil-A is closed on Sundays. Always has been, always will be.
  • Team Culture — We're more than coworkers. Come be part of a team that genuinely cares about each other.
