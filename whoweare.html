Who We Are

A locally owned restaurant with a passion for people and community.

Meet Our Operator

Nancy and Jeff (spouse and all-around great guy) moved to Minnesota from Georgia in the summer of 2013 to open Chick-fil-A in Maple Grove, one of the first freestanding locations in the state. Both are proud Air Force veterans, who love this country and were honored to serve.

Nancy was born in Indiana but raised around the world. As an Army brat she lived in several states, Germany and South Korea. Nancy loves sports but rarely plays them in her old age. She is also an ordained Lutheran pastor who enjoys helping people understand the grace of God and God’s love for all. She has a heart for people. Jeff was born in Ohio but raised in Georgia. He loves sports too but like Nancy he doesn’t play much anymore, just watches. Both are big SEC football fans. Go DAWGS! Jeff is a retired Delta pilot who now helps in the restaurant with maintenance and other stuff.

Jeff and Nancy have two children, twins Jon and Jamie, born in San Antonio, Texas. Jon lives with his partner Joe in Berlin Germany and works for the German company, SAP. Jamie lives with her husband Jeremy in Savage, Minnesota. She works as a transplant nurse at Abbot Northwestern Hospital. They have two children, Henry and Annie, who are Jeff and Nancy's grandkids.

Our Values

To glorify God by being a faithful steward of all that is entrusted to us. To have a positive influence on all who come in contact with Chick-fil-A.

We believe that a strong and caring culture is essential to our success. Chick-fil-A is committed to a workplace culture where everyone is treated with honor, dignity and respect.

We model our Purpose every day, supporting each other's efforts to be good stewards who create a positive impact.

We Pursue What's Next

Our History

Chick-fil-A was founded in 1967 by Truett Cathy in Hapeville, Georgia, with one simple idea: serve the best chicken sandwich anyone had ever tasted. Since then, Chick-fil-A has grown to thousands of locations nationwide, each one locally operated and deeply connected to its community.

Our Maple Grove location carries on that legacy every day, one guest, one meal, one moment at a time.
